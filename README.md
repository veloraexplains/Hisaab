# Hisaab — हिसाब
### Your Digital Ledger for Indian Small Businesses

---

## What is Hisaab?

Hisaab is a **free, offline-first** digital ledger (khata book) app designed for **Indian small business owners** — kirana stores, chai stalls, tailors, mechanics, vegetable vendors, and anyone who gives credit to customers.

Replace your paper khata with a fast, private, and reliable digital version.

---

## Why Hisaab?

- **63 million MSMEs in India** still use paper ledgers
- Paper gets lost, damaged, or unreadable
- Business owners lose ₹50,000–₹5,00,000/year in forgotten debts
- Existing apps are heavy (30+ MB), need internet, or take commissions

**Hisaab is different:**
- ✅ 100% Offline — no internet needed, ever
- ✅ Tiny APK Under  (10~MB)
- ✅ Hindi + English + 4 More languages 
- ✅ Zero commissions
- ✅ No login, no ads, no tracking

---

## Core Features

1. Add customers with name + phone
2. Record "You gave" (उधार दिया) / "You got" (भुगतान मिला)
3. See full ledger per customer
4. View total pending amount
5. Send WhatsApp/SMS reminders
6. Export/backup data to phone
7. PIN lock for privacy
8. Hindi + English toggle
9. Search customers
10. Works offline always

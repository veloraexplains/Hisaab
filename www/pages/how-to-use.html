How to Use

Follow these simple steps to start using Hisaab
— from setup to sending bills

1
Set Up Your Shop
Go to Settings → 🏪 Business Info and enter:
• Shop Name
• Owner Name
• Phone Number
• Address
• UPI ID (for QR payments)
• GSTIN (if registered)
💡 This info appears on every PDF statement automatically.
2
Add Your First Customer
From home, tap the green + button at bottom-right. Enter customer name and phone (optional). Tap Save.
💡 You can add unlimited customers. All data stays on your phone.
3
Record an Entry
Tap customer name → tap + button → fill entry:
You Gave — when customer takes items on credit
You Got — when customer pays money

You can fill both in one save — like when customer takes ₹100 of items and pays ₹100 cash.
💡 Tap "🍬 Add Item Details" to add items (Sugar 2kg, Aata 5kg). Optional but useful.
4
See Balance & Ledger
Tap any customer to see their full ledger:
• Red = Customer owes you
• Green = Customer paid
• Grey = Fully settled
5
Send WhatsApp Reminders
On customer page, tap 💬 to send WhatsApp reminder. Message includes:
• Your shop name
• Customer's pending amount
• Direct UPI payment link
💡 Go to Settings → 📢 Bulk Reminders to remind ALL pending customers at once.
6
Generate PDF Bill / Statement
On customer page, tap 📄 to generate PDF:
• Your shop header with address + GSTIN
• Customer name and phone
• All items and prices
• Payment received
• Pending balance
• QR code for instant UPI payment
💡 The PDF shows on-screen. Tap 📤 to send via WhatsApp or email.
7
Check Business Reports
Go to Settings → 📊 Reports to see:
• Today / Yesterday / Last 7 days / Last 30 days
• Custom date (pick any day)
• Given, Received, Net
• Top debtors and payers
8
Backup Your Data
Very important! Go to Settings → 💾 Backup and tap Backup Now every week.
The backup file saves to your phone storage. Also upload it to Google Drive for safety.
⚠️ If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, you can restore from backup.
9
Switch Language
Go to Settings → 🌐 Language to switch between:
• English
• हिंदी
• தமிழ்
• తెలుగు
• मराठी
10
Set PIN Lock (Optional)
Go to Settings → 🔒 PIN Lock and set a 4-digit PIN. Only you can open the app.
💡 Pro Tip: Use Hisaab every day. Add entries as they happen. Backup weekly. That's it — your khata is always up to date.

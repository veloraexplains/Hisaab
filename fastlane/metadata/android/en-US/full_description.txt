Hisaab is a completely offline customer ledger (khata book) app designed
for small business owners — kirana stores, chai stalls, tailors, mechanics,
and anyone who gives credit to customers.

Replace your paper khata with a fast, private, and reliable digital version.

WHY HISAAB?
- 100% offline — no internet needed, ever
- Tiny APK (under 10 MB)
- Hindi + English + 4 more languages
- Zero commissions
- No login, no ads, no tracking

CORE FEATURES
- Add customers with name and phone
- Record credit ("You gave" / उधार दिया) and payment ("You got" / भुगतान मिला)
- See full ledger per customer
- View total pending amount
- Send WhatsApp/SMS reminders
- Export and backup data to your phone
- PIN lock for privacy
- Hindi + English toggle
- Search customers
- Works offline always

TARGET USERS
- Kirana store owners
- Chai stalls and small food vendors
- Tailors and dry cleaners
- Mechanics and repair shops
- Vegetable and fruit vendors
- Milk sellers
- Any small business giving credit

PRIVACY
The app requests ZERO internet permissions and works 100% offline.
No ads. No tracking. No accounts.
